Bug fix release.

## Fixes

- The app now reconnects when another app resets the YubiKey while it stays
  plugged in.
  Before, every unlock failed with "The YubiKey was removed" until you
  pulled the key out.
- Locking the vault now also clears the key's PIN-verified state, so the
  card is not left unlocked after the app locks.
- After the 5-minute auto-lock the panel shows the lock screen again,
  instead of an empty vault ("No items yet").

## Downloads

- `99passwords-0.1.1-macos-arm64.zip`: the menu bar app, for Macs with Apple
  silicon and macOS 14 or later. The Chrome extension is included inside.
- `99passwords-extension-0.1.1.zip`: the Chrome extension on its own
  (unchanged since 0.1.0).
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums of both files.

## Opening the app (it is not notarized)

The app is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the
first time. Quit the old version, replace `99passwords.app` in
Applications, open it, click **Done**, then click **Open Anyway** in
**System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/99passwords.app`
