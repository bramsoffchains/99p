First public release of 99passwords: a macOS password manager whose vault
lives only on your YubiKey 5.

## Downloads

- `99passwords-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip`: the menu bar app, for Macs with Apple
  silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later. The Chrome extension is
  included inside the app.
- `99passwords-extension-0.1.0.zip`: the same Chrome extension on its own.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums of both files.

## Opening the app (it is not notarized)

The app is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the
first time:

1. Unzip it and drag `99passwords.app` into Applications.
2. Open it. macOS says it cannot verify the app; click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
   **Open Anyway** next to 99passwords, then confirm.

Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/99passwords.app`

If you prefer, build it yourself from source with `macos/build-app.sh`.

## Installing the extension

Unzip the extension, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**
and click **Load unpacked** on the unzipped folder. The app also walks you
through this from its own setup screen.

## Before you start

Setting up a YubiKey takes over its PIV application (it gets a new PIN and
management key). OTP, FIDO2 and passkeys keep working. If you already use PIV
certificates on a key, use another one.
