Rename release: everything that was called "ykvault" is now "99p".

## Changes

- The admin CLI is now `99p` (`99p info`, `99p piv-reset SERIAL`).
- The browser bridge inside the app is now `99p-bridge`. The app rewrites
  Chrome's native messaging manifest at launch, so nothing to do there.
- Encrypted backups are now `.99p` files. Older `.ykv` backups are the same
  format: rename them to `.99p` to restore them.
- Your vault on the YubiKey is unchanged and opens as before.

## Updating

The app and the Chrome extension must be updated together, because the
messages between them were renamed. After installing the new app, open
`chrome://extensions` and reload 99passwords (or load the new unpacked
extension).

## Downloads

- `99passwords-0.1.2-macos-arm64.zip`: the menu bar app, for Macs with Apple
  silicon and macOS 14 or later. The Chrome extension is included inside.
- `99passwords-extension-0.1.2.zip`: the Chrome extension on its own.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums of both files.

## Opening the app (it is not notarized)

The app is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the
first time. Quit the old version, replace `99passwords.app` in
Applications, open it, click **Done**, then click **Open Anyway** in
**System Settings → Privacy & Security**.

Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/99passwords.app`
